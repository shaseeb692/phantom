# Phantom Marketing — Keywords, Content Types & Tone

## 1. Content Objective

Phantom Marketing content must achieve four goals:

1. Explain the service clearly.
2. Build topical and commercial search relevance.
3. Support AEO, GEO and LLM / AI Search discovery.
4. Convert qualified visitors.

Content should never sound like generic AI-written agency copy.

---

## 2. Brand Tone

Primary tone:

- Confident
- Sharp
- Premium
- Strategic
- Technically credible
- Commercially focused
- Slightly rebellious

Secondary Phantom flavor:

- Dark
- Supernatural
- Playful
- Mysterious
- Powerful

Core rule:

**Clarity first. Phantom personality second.**

---

## 3. What to Avoid

Avoid:

- Keyword stuffing
- Generic agency filler
- Robotic SEO writing
- Fake statistics
- Unsupported claims
- Excessive "best", "#1", "leading"
- Repetitive supernatural metaphors
- Long meaningless introductions
- Repeating the same paragraph across service pages
- Parent/child content duplication

---

## 4. Content Types

### Parent / Main Service Pages

Intent:

Broad commercial and category authority.

Cover:

- What the service is
- Business problem
- Capabilities
- Sub-services
- Methodology
- Process
- Common failure points
- Markets
- Industries
- Tools/platforms
- Business outcomes
- FAQs
- CTA

Parent pages establish breadth.

---

### Child Service Pages

Intent:

Focused commercial service intent.

Cover:

- Specific service definition
- Use cases
- Business value
- Specific capabilities
- Specific process
- Common problems
- Relevant FAQs
- Relationship to parent service

Child pages establish depth.

Child pages must NOT duplicate parent copy.

Child pages must NOT display Digital Arsenal.

---

### Blog Content

Possible intent:

- Informational
- How-to
- Problem solving
- Comparison
- Commercial investigation
- Industry update
- Trend analysis
- Definitions
- Explainers

Blogs should support service topical clusters.

Use contextual internal links to relevant service pages.

---

### Case Studies

Recommended structure:

1. Situation
2. Problem
3. Objective
4. Strategy
5. Execution
6. Result
7. Business Impact
8. Lessons / Insight

Never fabricate results.

## 10. Keyword Usage Rules

Every page should have:

- One clear primary topic
- One clear search intent
- Natural primary keyword use
- Relevant secondary terms
- Related entities
- Relevant platforms
- Business problems
- Business outcomes

Primary keywords may naturally appear in:

- Metadata
- H1
- Opening section
- Relevant headings
- Body content
- FAQs

Do not force exact-match repetition.

---

## 11. Parent vs Child SEO Strategy

Parent:

**Category breadth**

Child:

**Specialist depth**

Example:

Google Ads parent explains the complete Google Ads ecosystem.

Search Ads child deeply explains Search Ads.

The Search Ads page should not simply reproduce the Google Ads parent content.

This reduces keyword cannibalization and improves topical structure.

---

## 12. AEO / GEO / LLM Writing Rules

Write content that remains useful when extracted from the page.

Prefer:

- Direct definitions
- Direct answers
- Descriptive headings
- Clear relationships
- Concise lists where appropriate
- Useful FAQs
- Named technologies/platforms
- Explicit problem -> method -> result explanations

Avoid artificial AI-search filler.

Useful, accurate and structured content is the foundation.

---

## 13. CTA Tone

CTA copy should combine conversion clarity with Phantom personality.

Possible direction:

- Conjure Your Next Growth Move
- Step Out of the Digital Shadows
- Make Your Brand Impossible to Ignore
- Unleash Your Digital Presence
- HIT THAT DAMN BUTTON!

CTA wording must still relate to the actual service.
