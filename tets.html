Whispers from the Void: FAQs

Have questions about PPC? We've got answers that cut through the digital fog.

What is PPC and how does it work?

PPC (Pay-Per-Click) is an online advertising model where advertisers pay a fee each time one of their ads is clicked. Essentially, it’s a way of buying visits to your site, rather than attempting to “earn” those visits organically. It works by setting up campaigns with specific keywords, demographics, and ad creatives, then bidding on ad placements.

How long does it take to see results from PPC?

One of the main advantages of PPC is its speed. You can start seeing traffic and conversions almost immediately after launching a campaign. However, optimal performance and significant ROI typically require a few weeks to a few months of continuous optimization, A/B testing, and data analysis.

What is a good ROI for PPC?

A good ROI (Return on Investment) for PPC varies significantly by industry, business model, and campaign goals. A common benchmark for a good ROI in Google Ads is 2:1, meaning you get $2 back for every $1 spent. However, for some businesses, a 4:1 or even higher ROI is achievable. Our goal is always to maximize your specific ROI based on your objectives.
